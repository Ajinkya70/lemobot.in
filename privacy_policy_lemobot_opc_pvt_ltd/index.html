Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lemobot (OPC) Private Limited collects, uses and protects information shared through our website, contact forms, WhatsApp links and project discussions.

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, company details, project requirements, website information and any message you send through our forms, email or WhatsApp. We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, page visits and referral source through standard analytics tools.

How We Use Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, understand project requirements, prepare proposals, provide services, maintain communication, improve our website and support ongoing client work. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing and Service Providers

We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when required for communication, hosting, analytics, project delivery or legal compliance. These parties are expected to handle information responsibly and only for the required purpose.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can request access, correction or deletion of information you have shared with us, subject to legal and business record requirements. You can also ask us to stop contacting you for non-essential communication.
